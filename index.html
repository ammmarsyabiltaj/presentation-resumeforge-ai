GRADUATE PAIN POINTS

Generic Resumes
One-size-fits-all applications fail ATS keyword filters, resulting in automatic rejection before human review.
No Feedback Loop
Zero insight into why applications are rejected. Graduates cannot improve what they cannot measure.
Low Conversion
Information asymmetry — candidates miss employer-specific needs like tailored skills framing and storytelling.
Access Inequality
Professional resume coaching concentrated in Klang Valley. 250–700 applicants per JobStreet posting.

EMPLOYER PAIN POINTS

Volume Overload
Manual screening is time-intensive and costly. Even with ATS, applicant volumes overwhelm hiring teams.
Poor Alignment
Keyword-based ATS misses contextual fit. Qualified candidates filtered out for wrong optimisation.
Skill Mismatch
Structural oversupply in certain fields means high volumes of misaligned applications per role.
Slow Hiring
Prolonged cycles despite screening tools. Nuanced qualification assessment remains a human bottleneck.
